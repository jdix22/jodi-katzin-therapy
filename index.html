Questions

Before you reach out

The short answers to what women usually want to know first.

Who do you work with?

Women in midlife who are navigating identity shifts, changing family roles, big emotions, feeling stuck or overwhelmed, a search for meaning, or body image and eating struggles.

Do you offer virtual sessions?

Yes. Sessions are available virtually or in-person in Vaughan, Ontario.

How much does a session cost?

Individual sessions are 50 minutes and $150 per session. The first 15-minute consultation is free.

Is therapy covered by my insurance?

If you have extended health coverage for Occupational Therapy, I am able to provide a receipt for reimbursement.

How soon will I hear back?

Reach out anytime at jodikatzin@yahoo.com and I will get back to you within 1–2 business days.
